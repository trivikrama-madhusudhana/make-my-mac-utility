All options: one project, local saving, confirmation before reset, and one-step undo (including reset). Editing the name saves when you leave the field or press Return; an empty name becomes “My knitting.” Undo history lasts until you quit. The native app will have menu-bar Show, Hide and Quit, no Dock icon, and an ordinary window that other windows can cover. These demos cannot demonstrate native window behavior.
