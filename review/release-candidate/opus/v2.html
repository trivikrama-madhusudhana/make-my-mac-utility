Steep: three designs · revision 3

All three designs have the same features. You type minutes and seconds, then press Start. The panel counts down and then shows a big Ready that stays until you dismiss it. A cup in the menu bar shows, hides or quits Steep. There are no sounds or notifications. The panels float over a pretend document, and you can drag them.

Your pick so far: A · Mug (unchanged at 240 × 288). B and C are kept for comparison and have the same changes.
Changed in revision 3: Cancel and Dismiss can now fail too. Under each design, the Pretend saving fails when I press switches have Cancel and Dismiss next to Start. If Cancel can't be saved, the countdown keeps going. If Dismiss can't be saved, Ready stays. Either way the panel says what didn't happen, and the button becomes Try again. Turn the switch off and press Try again to finish the action.

This is a simulation in your browser. The timers, menu bar and windows aren't real. Only the finished Mac app can show how it floats, moves between desktops and handles focus.
