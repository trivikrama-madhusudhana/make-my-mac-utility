A little time for tea.

Three directions for Steep, your quiet floating tea timer. Type minutes and seconds, then Start. Each preview runs locally and silently. These frames illustrate appearance; dragging, floating and the real Mac menu bar come with the native app.

A · Hearth — recommended

A centered timer, easy to catch at a glance. Takes a little more vertical room than the strip.

B · Tea strip

Entry and action sit side by side. Low enough to tuck near an edge, but wider on your desk.

C · Tea card

A friendly, prominent heading with time beneath it. More character and a slightly taller footprint.

Preview tools below each frame simulate Ready, a storage failure and menu-bar actions. Quit here only hides the demo; it does not close your browser. No data is saved. Native Steep will remember a brew’s actual finish time through sleep and quitting. No sound, notifications or automatic startup.

All three render and interaction reviews are uninspected in this CLI environment. Please check your preferred option’s inputs, Start, Cancel and Ready → Dismiss. At narrow widths, the wider strip can scroll horizontally without shrinking its controls.
