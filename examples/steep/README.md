# Steep: a tea timer for your Mac

Set a steeping time, start the timer, and leave it visible while you work. When the time is up, a large Ready message stays until you dismiss it. A cup in the menu bar gives you a way to show, hide, or quit the app. This example uses no sounds or notifications.

Open [options.html](options.html) in your browser to try three designs: Mug, Strip, and Dial. The Mug design was selected during the evaluation. Try a short countdown, or enable Fast demo to see the timer finish sooner. The controls below each design also let you simulate failures when saving or reading a timer.

This is the actual revision 4 preview generated by Opus in Claude Code during the September 13 evaluation, preserved without changes. Its selection buttons prepare a choice summary; they do not connect to an agent session. The current skill includes a separate browser-to-agent review helper.

The preview simulates the app's behavior in memory. Its menu bar, windows, quit/reopen, and time jumps are demonstrations; it does not install a Mac app or preserve a real timer across browser reloads. The native Steep app was also built and exercised during evaluation. Its keyboard, save/read recovery, and quit/relaunch results are recorded in the [four-model evaluation](../../evaluation/RELEASE-CANDIDATE-RESULTS.md), together with repairs and limits.

This is one example of what the skill can help build. Its style and functionality are not requirements for your own utility.
